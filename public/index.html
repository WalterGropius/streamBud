    Skip the copy-paste: share straight from the app

    Android

    1. Open this page in Chrome, tap ⋮ → Add to Home screen (or Install app).
    2. In Spotify or YouTube Music, tap Share → streamBud.
    3. The other link is ready to copy (or already copied).

    iPhone

    iOS doesn’t let web apps into the share sheet, but a one-minute Shortcut does the same thing:

    1. Open the Shortcuts app → +. Tap the ⓘ and turn on Show in Share Sheet.
    2. Add the action URL Encode (it encodes the Shortcut Input).
    3. Add Open URLs with this, putting the URL Encoded Text at the end:
      https://your-streambud-site/?text=
    4. Name it streamBud. Now tap Share → streamBud in Spotify or YouTube Music.

    Tip: tap Share → Add to Home Screen in Safari to keep streamBud one tap away.
